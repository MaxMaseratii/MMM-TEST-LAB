The account machine: how losing rules still post big payouts

A trader copies one rule onto many prop accounts, rebuys every account that blows up, and posts the total of all payouts. The simulation below does exactly that with each trader's real backtest trades, and with random trades (no edge at all).

Assumed generic 50k account: pass at +$3,000 before a $2,500 trailing drawdown, $1,000 daily loss stop; funded payouts of everything above a $2,600 buffer every 8 trading days (min $500, 100% of the first $25k then 90%); a blown account is replaced at once. All accounts take the same trades (a trade copier), started 5 trading days apart. Risk per trade = the trader's own size. Generic rows include no activation fee. Apex-like rows: $25 evaluation + $79 activation per funded account, payouts capped at $2,000 each and at most 6 payouts per account before it must be replaced. No consistency rule is modelled, so real payouts would be a bit lower.
