Gamma Walls @MaxMaserati

Options gamma walls on your chart. Built for NQ / MNQ and ES / MES.
No signals, no arrows, no bias. Just the levels.

WHAT IT SHOWS
• 90-Day walls: call wall, put wall and zero gamma. Type them in, or leave 0 and the indicator estimates them.
• Today's levels (0DTE): paste today's strikes and you get today's call wall, put wall, GEX flip and the next biggest strikes (magnets).
• Index → futures: NDX / SPX levels are moved onto your NQ / ES chart with the live futures-index gap.
• Gamma histogram next to price: calls green, puts red, quiet pockets grey.
• Level map: every level with its distance from price in points and a short name (ceiling, floor, magnet).

MARKET TYPE
• CALM: price is above the flip (or zero gamma). Walls tend to hold.
• FAST: price is below it. Moves tend to stretch.

HUD
• MARKET: calm or fast, and where price sits between the 90D walls.
• ABOVE / BELOW: the nearest wall or magnet above and below price.
Compact or Detailed mode (Detailed adds every level).

HOW TO USE
1. Add it to an NQ, MNQ, ES or MES chart.
2. Optional: type the 90-day walls and paste today's strikes (strike:value per line, minus sign for put bars).
3. Use the walls as a map. Take entries from your own setup.

NOTES
• Typed or pasted levels only show from today's 18:00 New York open, so history never shows levels you could not have known.
• Auto walls are estimates from volume, marked "est.".
