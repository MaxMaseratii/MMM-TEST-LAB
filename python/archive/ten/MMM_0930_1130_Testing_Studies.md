MMM research desk · updated Oct 28 2026 · NQ 1m Dec 2022–Dec 2025 (763 days) / MNQ 1m 2026 (61 days). Every cell = 2022–25 / 2026.

## 0. The data and the rules — Read this first

- **2022–25** = NQ 1-minute data, Dec 2022 to Dec 2025, **763 trading days**. This is where the numbers come from.
- **2026** = MNQ 1-minute data, Mar–May and Sep–Oct 2026, **61 days**. A fresh check. Small sample: treat 2026 as a guess when n is under 30.
- Every result is written **2022–25 / 2026**. Example: 83 / 85% = 83% in 2022–25 and 85% in 2026.
- All times are New York. Weekdays only. Days with a missing 1m bar between 09:30 and 09:59 were skipped.
- The window ends at **11:30**, the moment Max stops trading.
- Candles are read with **MMM Candle Bias**: each candle compared with the one before it. TGT (target) = the lean-side extreme. Protected = the other end.
- Parts 1 to 8 count what price does. They are **not** tested strategies. Part 9 is a real trade test with a 1 point cost.

## 10. Best read per timeframe

**4H**
- A BC+ at 10:00: target first 87–90% (75–78% in 2026).
- Weak 4H candle: the gap behind it comes first 52%. Wait there.
- Pullback when the lean is right: median 34 / 65 pts, mostly before 10:30.

**1h**
- BC+: target first 86–91%.
- Weak 1h candle: gap or target is a coin flip.

**15m**
- The best balance. BC+: target first 89–92%.
- Runs after 10:00: about half still alive at 11:30.
- 20 pts reached 70 / 87%, 30 pts 59 / 73%.

**5m**
- The fastest. BC+: target first 86–89%.
- Opening gaps react 68 / 79% with the 4H lean.
- Runs end in 25–35 minutes. 20 pts reached 68 / 80%.

## 11. How to use it from 09:30 to 11:30 — A reading order built from these counts, not a tested strategy

1. **Before 09:30** — Note the 4H 06:00–10:00 candle forming and any open UPG gaps from the night. The 4H side is the filter for the opening gaps.
2. **09:30–10:00** — Expect the morning high or low to be made here (about 1 day in 3 in the first 15 minutes). Read the 09:30 5m / 15m candle: a BC+ shows the first push. Mark the 09:30–10:00 range.
3. **09:30–10:00 gaps** — A 5m gap that points WITH the 4H candle reacts 68 / 79% of the time. Against it, it fails more often than it holds.
4. **10:00** — Read the candles that close at 10:00. BC+ → its target is likely next. BC− → no side. Weak 1h or 4H candle → price often visits the open gap behind it first.
5. **10:00–11:30** — Count the run: about 2 BCs and 2–3 NBCs, then the other side closes. Take points (20–30 on 5m / 15m) before the opposite candle. Use "close past the BC low" as the line that ends the run.
6. **11:30** — Stop. Most mornings already made their range, and the 10:00 lean says nothing about 16:00.

## 12. What does NOT work

- The 10:00 lean to call the 16:00 close (about 50–60%).
- A BC− candle for direction (coin flip).
- Holding until the opposite candle: the run gives the points back (average about 0 at the exit).
- The first BC after 10:00 as an entry with a 50 pt stop at 1:2 (no edge, t-score under 2).
- Guessing the side of the opening range before the first close out of it (about 50/50).

## 13. Words used

- **BC** = Body close: the candle closes past the previous candle’s high (bull) or low (bear). Urgency.
- **NBC** = No body close: the candle closes inside the previous candle. Negotiation.
- **BC+ / BC−** = A BC that closes in its strong quarter (+) or not (−).
- **C2** = The candle takes the other side first, then closes through: an engulf.
- **AFs / CI / S&D** = Sweep and close back inside / inside candle / takes both sides and closes inside.
- **TGT / protected** = TGT = the lean-side extreme of the candle. Protected = the other end.
- **UPG gap** = A 3-candle gap: candle 3 never comes back to candle 1’s high (bull).
- **MFE** = The most points in our favour before the end.
- **Opening range** = The high and low of 09:30–10:00.
