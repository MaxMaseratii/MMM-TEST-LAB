# MMM XB Range Trade Odds (Oct 30 2026)

Question (Max): if I enter INSIDE a live Xpander Block at a zone (sell XBB / buy XBS) and target the other side (other side of the Balanced Zone, the other zone, the other XB edge), what are the odds of a violation, and how big is the drawdown?

## Answer

- **The odds are high, the money is not.** Zone edge (XBB bottom = Balanced top / XBS top = Balanced bottom) → other side of the Balanced Zone hits before a violation 66–81% of the time (15m to daily, both periods).
- **The loss is about 3× the win.** 15m: the target is a median 6 pts away; a violation close costs a median 19 pts (31 in 2026). 1h: 12 vs 44. So the average trade loses on 15m and 1h with every exit (target / violation close, stop 2 ticks past the XB edge, 50 pt stop).
- **4h and daily** are near break-even. Daily zone edge → other side of Balanced made +17.7 → +28.9 pts a trade (t 1.4 / 0.7, only 156 / 46 trades): not proven.
- **The XB edge (XB high / low) is where the block breaks.** Price that touches it closes through it before reaching the Balanced middle 46–59% of the time, and before the other XB edge 71–78%. A stop 2 ticks past the XB edge after entering AT the edge is hit almost every time (99%).
- **Deeper = lower odds.** Zone edge → other XB edge: 50–61%. Zone middle → other XB edge: 35–41%.
- **Entering at the Balanced middle** (the "no-trade" zone) to its other side hits 74–94%, but only because the target is 3–6 pts (15m) away; it also loses on average.
- **Drawdown before a winner** (zone edge → other side of Balanced, median / 90%): 15m 4 / 21 pts (2026: 12 / 46) · 1h 8 / 43 (17 / 71) · 4h 12 / 79 (34 / 127) · daily 32 / 199 (36 / 240).

## Method

- XB engine: opposite side only (MMM PRO x 4.0 default). Balanced Zone = top / bottom of the opens / closes inside the XB. Levels follow the block as it grows; targets are fixed at the touch.
- Entries: zone edge (Balanced top for sells / bottom for buys), zone middle, XB edge, and the Balanced middle as reference. Sells and buys are mirrors and are added together.
- A level counts only on a TRUE touch: the 1m bar opens on the right side and trades through it.
- Violation = a structure candle closes beyond the XB edge against the trade (the block breaks against you). Hit = the target printed on a later 1m bar before that close.
- Drawdown = the worst move against the entry (pts) before the target hit (winners only).
- Exits for the money: target or the violation close · stop 2 ticks past the XB edge · 50 pt stop. 1 pt cost.
- Data: NQ 1m Dec 2022 – Dec 2025 (P1) → MNQ 2026 (P2). Timeframes 15m, 1h, 4h, daily (session 18:00 → 17:00).
